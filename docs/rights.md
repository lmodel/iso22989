# Licensing And Standards Rights

This page is for anyone who wants to reuse this repository or the standard's text: it says what the project's licence covers and what it does not.

The Apache-2.0 licence covers the original material in this repository: the schema's structure, the source code and the project's documentation.

ISO and IEC hold the copyright in ISO/IEC 22989. ISO offers it free of charge from its Publicly Available Standards page, under its Customer Licence Agreement, and that agreement also restricts use of the text with AI tools.

The project's licence grants no right to reproduce, redistribute or sublicense ISO's text. When you use this schema in a tool or a data pipeline, write your own descriptions or refer to the licensed text, and embed the standard's text only with ISO's permission. [OVERLAY.md](https://github.com/lmodel/iso22989/blob/main/src/iso22989/schema/OVERLAY.md) says how a licence holder keeps that text in a private copy of the schema.

The repository keeps ISO's own metadata records of the edition and its pending amendments, from ISO Open Data, under the ODC Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0, and [NOTICE](https://github.com/lmodel/iso22989/blob/main/NOTICE) gives the attribution. It leaves out each record's scope, which summarizes the standard in ISO's words.
