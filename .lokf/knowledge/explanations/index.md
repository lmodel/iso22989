# Explanations

* [Modelling choices](modelling-choices.md) - Why the schema has these shapes: a vocabulary a host can describe an AI system with, records that refer by identifier, hierarchies with a designator rather than kind fields, and no class without a clause or term behind it.
* [Mappings and the SSSOM sets](mappings.md) - Where the schema crosses to other vocabularies: inline mappings to released gist, SPDX and UCO classes, each tested. The nine SSSOM sets are gone, since a matching tool wrote them and most targets no longer exist.
* [Scope](scope.md) - What the schema leaves out: the standard's text, its abbreviations and its pending amendments. Its OECD pairings are this project's reading of Annex A, not checked against its text.
