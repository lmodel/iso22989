# Datasets

* [The schema's adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - `.lokf/iso22989.yaml`, the copy `just lokf-adapt` writes from the schema so that it sits beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml`: fifteen classes rooted on `Concept`, `ai_rmf_id` dropped for LOKF's `id`, `title` and `description` folded into LOKF's, and two designators demoted.
* [The iso22989 LinkML schema](iso22989-linkml-schema.md) - `src/iso22989/schema/iso22989.yaml`: the vocabulary for describing an AI system in ISO/IEC 22989's terms, with the `Aic` class prefix, the `aic_` slot prefix and `AicEcosystem` as its root.
* [The term and clause numbers](term-numbers.md) - `tests/iso22989_numbers.yaml`: every clause number of the standard's table of contents, and each term of Clause 3 with the element that renders it or why none does. It holds numbers and element names only.
* [ISO Open Data's records](iso-open-data-records.md) - `src/iso22989/schema/iso-open-data/`: ISO's records of the edition and its two pending amendments as of 2026-11-05, under ODC-By v1.0, each without its scope and pinned by hash.
* [The overlay scaffold](overlay.md) - `src/iso22989/schema/iso22989-overlay.template.yaml`: an empty scaffold of every element, which a licence holder copies and fills privately, and merges into a private schema under `tmp/`.
* [The generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project` and `just gen-doc` generate from the schema: the projections in `project/`, the Python models in `src/iso22989/datamodel/`, and the merged schema in `docs/schema/`. None is edited by hand.
* [The example data](example-data.md) - `tests/data/`: a valid example per class and a counter-example per constraint, each counter-example naming on its first line where it must fail. `AicEcosystem-card-payments` is one whole ecosystem.
* [A bank as AI producer](example-stakeholder.md) - An example written as a concept of type `AicStakeholder` in the adapt copy's keys. It shows that the domain schema admits a party as a concept, with its role and sub-role.
* [The neighbours' adapt copies](neighbour-copies.md) - The adapt copies of 19 released lmodel repositories in `tests/neighbours/`, each pinned by its SHA-256, which tests hold this schema's names and mappings to.
